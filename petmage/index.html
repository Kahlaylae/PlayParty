About Pet Mage: No Hands

Pet Mage: No Hands is a fast-paced fantasy action game where an armless hooded mage is trapped inside a small terrarium, endlessly battling hordes of monsters spawning from unstable portals. With relentless wave-based combat, players unleash powerful magic to survive constant enemy swarms — including goblins and massive creatures.

Aided by a fire-orb-throwing pixie and a summoned badger companion, gameplay focuses on nonstop arena action, enemy clearing, and survival against overwhelming odds. Built for high-intensity combat fans, Pet Mage: No Hands delivers chaotic magical mayhem and continuous monster waves inside a confined, ever-active arena.
