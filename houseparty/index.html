Free Multiplayer Party Games Online

Welcome to House Party — Play Party Online's flagship collection of multiplayer games designed for the living room.

Imagine Jackbox-style party games, but free and browser-based. Cast to your TV, use phones as controllers, and play with everyone in the room.

How House Party Games Work

  • Host opens the game on a TV or big screen
  • Players join with their phones by scanning a code or entering a room URL
  • No downloads, no accounts, no apps — just play
  • Supports 2-8+ players depending on the game

What to Expect

We're building party games that are funny, competitive, and perfect for game nights. Trivia, drawing games, voting games, and chaotic minigame collections.

House Party games are designed for:

  • Family game nights
  • Friends hanging out
  • Parties and gatherings
  • Stream-friendly content
